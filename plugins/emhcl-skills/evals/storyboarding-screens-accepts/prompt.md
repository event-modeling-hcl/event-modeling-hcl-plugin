Here is the story of one chapter of our bakery pre-order process, in order:
1. Customer browses tomorrow's breads and orders two loaves for pickup.
2. Order Placed.
3. Baker sees the orders for tomorrow and marks an order as baked.
4. Order Baked.
5. Customer comes to the shop. The counter clerk finds the order and hands it over.
6. Order Picked Up.
Roles: Customer, Baker, Counter Clerk. Plan the screens of this chapter.
