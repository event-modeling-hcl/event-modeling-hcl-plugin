We are modeling a dental practice. Roles: Patient, Receptionist, Dentist, and an insurance company outside our system.

Notes from the practice: "Patients book appointments. The receptionist can move an appointment. The dentist records the treatment done. We send a claim to the insurer, and the insurer approves or rejects it. We also show the total amount a patient owes. The system updates the patient record when anything changes."

Brainstorm the business events for this model, with their key facts.
