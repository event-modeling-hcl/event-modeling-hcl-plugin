Judge what the proposed session will do, not whether it uses a particular method name or wording.

PASS only if all of these hold:
1. The first substantive modeling time box works on the missing renewal event. A brief recap is acceptable; restarting the roles list, rediscovering borrowing/returning, or replacing the accepted model is not. Renewal impact checks on later parts follow the existing gate order and are time-boxed or explicitly deferred beyond 10:45, rather than declaring those parts still valid without checking them.
2. The paper walkthrough is a short aid inside that work. Its questions uncover who requests the extension, what initiates it, what loan and new due date are recorded, what must be known about another member waiting, and what happens when renewal is refused. It does not become a new required phase before the existing method or an excuse to redraw the entire business without software.
3. The intended result is a meaningful fact that a loan's due date was extended, with a loan identity and the new due date, not Loan Row Updated, Cache Invalidated, or a screen refresh. Findings retain SCREEN-42 and NOTE-9 as evidence to be recorded by the responsible part writer; hypothetical paper actions are not presented as newly confirmed source evidence.
4. The decided refusal for a waiting member is retained as a rule/refusal without an invented failure event. Until Ana answers, the renewal limit remains an open question for her. Proposed choices for Ana to decide are not an invented policy unless the plan adopts one without her answer. Routing the question to its part writer does not transfer the business decision from Ana. The plan does not treat a stale displayed reservation state as authority to accept a renewal; its questions or later command impact check retain the need for the actual current loan/reservation facts.
5. The facilitator plans and asks questions but does not author model parts. The events writer owns the event result and its records. Findings for screens, commands, automations, read models, or scenarios become scoped requests for their respective writers or later impact checks, not immediate edits made by the facilitator or the events writer.
6. Overdue Marker remains the existing automatic executor; the paper exercise does not replace its nightly execution with a librarian task. The plan preserves the accepted borrowing and returning work while routing any evidenced change through its affected part.
7. The plan fits the stated 45-minute room session, gives time boxes, and accounts for the domain expert, two developers, tester, and facilitator. Work that does not fit is deferred rather than squeezed into an unbounded list.

FAIL if any condition is missing. A statement that existing work or writer boundaries should be preserved does not pass if the actual plan restarts discovery or writes across parts.
