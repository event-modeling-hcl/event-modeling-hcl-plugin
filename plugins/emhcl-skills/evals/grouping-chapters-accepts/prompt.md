Events of a gym model, in no order:

Member Joined, Membership Card Issued, Class Booked, Class Booking Canceled, Class Attended, Monthly Fee Charged, Fee Charge Failed, Trainer Hired, Trainer Schedule Set, Guest Day Pass Bought, Guest Entered.

A guest pays at the door for one day, has no account, and uses other screens than members. A fee charge succeeds or fails, and the rest is the same.

Group these events into chapters.
